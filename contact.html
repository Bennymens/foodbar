What the faq

Do you deliver nationwide?
Yes! We deliver our delicious plantain chips and coated groundnuts across the country. Just contact us for bulk orders or find us in your local supermarket.
What events do you cater for?
We cater for all events ranging from weddings, corporate parties, birthdays, to picnics. Choose from our wide selection of local snacks!
Are your chips vegan-friendly?
Absolutely! Our snacks contain simple, honest plant-based ingredients. Plantains, salt, and spices.
Can I order in bulk?
Yes, we offer bulk pricing for retailers, offices, and large private events. Contact us via the form above for a custom quote and volume discounts.
What snacks do you offer?
Our core products are premium, naturally-flavored plantain chips (sweet and savory) and honey-roasted or salt-coated groundnuts.
What is the shelf life of your products?
Our snacks are made fresh without artificial preservatives and have a shelf life of up to 6 months when stored in a cool, dry place.
