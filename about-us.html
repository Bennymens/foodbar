Our Story

Founded in 2022, Longdon Foods was born out of a simple idea at Capitol Catering. What started as a delightful starter for buffets quickly caught the attention of our customers. People kept coming back, asking for extra plantain chips and coated nuts. The snacks became a favorite, not just as part of a meal, but treats tp enjoy on their own.

Encouraged by this love for our products, we decided to create a brand dedicated entirely to crafting these delicious snacks. Today, Longdon Foods is proud to offer high-quality plantain chips and coated nuts, made with care and crafted to delight every snack lover.

Our Mission

To deliver nutritious, locally produced snacks designed for everyone and perfectly suited for corporate functions and social occasions of every kind.

What Makes Longdon Snacks Different

Our difference lies in our commitment to health and authenticity. We stay true to the rich heritage of Ghanaian snacks, using locally sourced ingredients that reflect our culture and traditions. Every product is rooted in genuine Ghanaian taste, prepared with care and respect for local food craftsmanship.

What sets us apart is our healthy twist. We thoughtfully select quality ingredients and apply careful production processes to ensure our plantain chips and coated nuts are not only delicious but also offer real nutritional value.

We believe snacking should be enjoyable without compromise, authentic in flavor, wholesome in content, and satisfying in every bite.

That balance between tradition and health is what makes Longdon Foods truly unique.

Brand Pitch

Longdon foods is a proudly locally owned Ghanaian brand dedicated to redefining snacking through wholesome, made-in-Ghana products. We stand at the intersection of tradtion and wellness, offering nutritious alternatives to imported, processed snacks.

In an era where consumers are shifting towards healthier choices, our plantain chips and coated nuts meet that demand with authentic Ghanaian flavor and carefully elected ingredients giving people a reason to choose better without sacrificing taste.

The global market for plantain chips is valued at approximately $500 million, and demand continues to grow as more people around the world embrace healthier snacking options. This shift presents a clear opportunity for Longdon Foods to expand both locally and internationally, capturing space in a market that is ripe for innovation and authenticity.

We are uniquely positioned to lead this change. A Ghanaian brand with roots in local heritage, a promise of health , and the vision to serve discerning consumers everywhere.

Jonathan Longdon

Founder of Longdon Foods
